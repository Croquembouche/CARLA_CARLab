# Brochure visual acceptance target

Reference: 2020 Lincoln MKZ brochure, PDF pages 3 and 4, ebony upholstery and walnut trim. Downloaded reference is in reference/Lincoln_US_MKZ_2020.pdf.

The previous six-camera runtime pass establishes asset integration, physics and capture, but does not establish a close visual match to the brochure.

The current refinement revises seat contours, headrest curvature, shoulder panel layout and rear center rake, and transfers leather grain and perforation normals into Unreal. These changes require fresh visual inspection before acceptance.

Comparison views must use the actual CARLA renderer, matching composition and soft daylight as closely as practical. Empty-cabin presentation hides only the temporary occupant and their worn belt in that test process. The normal vehicle retains both.

Remaining visual criteria: dashboard and console silhouette; continuous walnut and satin metal trim; seat and headrest contours; restrained leather highlights and perforation; headliner/roof depth and glass; coherent rear bench; complete belts and fittings; no clipping or shading discontinuities. A photograph-derived approximation must not be labeled exact OEM geometry.
