DLSS 5 experiment — community approximation

Actual CUDA inference on eight saved 1280 × 800 CARLA RGB frames, using standard and natural profiles at full effect strength. Each slider pairs an unchanged native frame with its enhanced derivative. This portable community implementation has not been verified equivalent to NVIDIA DLSS 5. These are independent stills; this study does not measure motion stability or cross-camera consistency.

The enhancement changes image appearance. It does not modify the MKZ mesh, materials, lighting, vehicle physics or sensor ground truth. Fine details and color changes should be checked against the native image and manufacturer reference.

What changed in the asset
  • Layered paint with a clear coat, preserving the comparison paint color.
  • Revised glass appearance and a more restrained wheel finish.
  • Repaired caliper color and texture seams.
  • New modeled hex fasteners, tire valves and brake machining rings.

The original body shape, tires, skeleton and four-wheel physics remain the basis of the variant. The CARLA RGB and Blender Cycles asset comparisons are unenhanced; neural processing appears only in the explicitly labeled DLSS experiment modes.
