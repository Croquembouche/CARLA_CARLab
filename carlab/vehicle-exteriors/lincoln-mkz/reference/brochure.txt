E N J OY H Y B R I D R E W A R D S

MKZ is a driver’s car with the desire to excite, and the ability to deliver. Combine the
Twin-Turbocharged 3.0-liter V6 engine1 with Intelligent all-wheel drive (AWD),1 and you’ll
have an impressive 400 horsepower 2 and 400 lb.-ft. of torque2 at your disposal. You
can even take control of the 6-speed SelectShift® automatic transmission by tapping
the steering wheel-mounted paddle shifters for a more exhilarating ride.

Twin-scroll turbocharging helps the standard Turbocharged 2.0-liter I-4 engine provide
245 horsepower 2 and 275 lb.-ft. of torque,2 while direct fuel injection and twin-independent
variable camshaft timing (Ti-VCT) help contribute to efficiency. Equipped with the 2.0-liter,
MKZ has an EPA-estimated rating of 31 hwy mpg.3 This engine is also paired with the 6-speed
SelectShift automatic transmission and front-wheel drive or Intelligent AWD.1

The luxuriously equipped MKZ Hybrid Reserve has an EPA-estimated rating of 42 city mpg.4 It can travel in purely electric mode
at speeds up to 85 mph, and switches seamlessly between its Atkinson-cycle gas engine and electric traction motor as needed.
It also charges its own lithium-ion battery – so there’s no need for you to plug it in. To help maximize efficiency, the Regenerative
Braking System can capture up to 90% of the brake energy that would normally be lost and repurpose it as power.5 Plus, the
SmartGauge® instrument cluster with EcoGuide is designed to help you drive efficiently.
